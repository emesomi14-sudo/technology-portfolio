# ReConnectSphere: Testing and Evaluation

## Testing Approach

The website was reviewed across its five main sections:

- Homepage
- Community
- Opportunities
- Report a Concern
- Safety and Privacy

The review focused on navigation, content clarity, visual consistency and whether important information could be found easily.

## Evaluation Criteria

The website was assessed against the following criteria:

1. **Navigation:** Are the five sections clearly organised and accessible?
2. **Readability:** Is the information presented in straightforward language?
3. **Visual consistency:** Are colours, typography and layouts consistent?
4. **Accessibility:** Is the content structured clearly for the intended audience?
5. **Safety:** Are support options explained clearly, with appropriate boundaries around the website's role?

## Limitations

The website is an independent project and has not been professionally audited. Its evaluation does not establish that it is suitable for every user or that it meets all accessibility standards.

It does not provide emergency support, private messaging or live chat. Users are directed towards relevant organisations where further assistance may be needed.

## Future Improvements

Further development could include:

- Testing with a wider range of young people from different backgrounds.
- Gathering structured feedback on navigation and readability.
- Conducting more detailed accessibility checks.
- Reviewing links and information regularly to keep them relevant.

## Conclusion

The evaluation provides a basis for reviewing the website against its original design priorities. Further user testing would help identify improvements and assess how effectively ReConnectSphere meets the needs of its intended audience.
