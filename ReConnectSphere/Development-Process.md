# ReConnectSphere: Development Process

## 1. Problem Identification

Explored the experiences of young people who had recently moved to the UK, identifying challenges involving social connections, cultural adjustment and access to information.

## 2. User Research

Used questionnaires, semi-structured interviews and an external organisation consultation to investigate user experiences and support needs.

## 3. Requirements Gathering

Analysed the research findings and identified the information and features the website should provide.

## 4. Design

Used Figma to explore website layouts, page structures and navigation.

## 5. Development

Built ReConnectSphere using WordPress, creating separate sections for community, opportunities, reporting concerns, and safety and privacy.

## 6. Evaluation

Reviewed the website's navigation, readability, visual consistency and information structure.

## 7. Future Development

Further user testing, accessibility reviews and content improvements could help strengthen the website.
