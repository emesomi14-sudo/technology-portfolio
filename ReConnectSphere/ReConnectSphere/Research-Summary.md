# ReConnectSphere: Research Summary

## Research Aim

To explore the experiences of young people who have recently moved to the UK and identify the challenges they face when adjusting to a new environment.

## Research Methods

- Initial questionnaire: 141 responses.
- Follow-up questionnaire: 10 first-generation immigrant respondents.
- Six completed semi-structured interviews.
- Additional consultation with a Bournemouth migrant and asylum support organisation.

## Key Findings

Research explored:
- Social connections and making friends.
- Language, accents and communication.
- Cultural identity and belonging.
- Access to education and opportunities.
- Awareness of available support.

The findings highlighted the importance of accessible information, representation, social connection and trustworthy support.

## How Research Informed the Website

The findings informed the development of ReConnectSphere's:
- UK life information.
- Community section.
- Opportunities section.
- Report a Concern page.
- Safety and Privacy information.

## Reflection

This research helped me understand the importance of gathering information directly from intended users before designing a digital solution. It also showed me how research findings can be translated into practical website requirements.
