# ReConnectSphere: Website Requirements

## Purpose
To provide young people who have recently moved to the UK with accessible information, relatable experiences and signposting to relevant support.

## Target Audience
Young people aged approximately 13–18 who have migrated to the UK.

## Functional Requirements

1. **Homepage:** Introduce ReConnectSphere and explain its purpose.
2. **Community:** Share experiences and stories that help young people feel represented.
3. **Opportunities:** Provide information about education, activities and opportunities.
4. **Report a Concern:** Explain how users can raise concerns and find appropriate support.
5. **Safety and Privacy:** Explain user safety, privacy and responsible website use.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Clear and consistent navigation.
- Accessible language and readable content.
- Respect for different cultural backgrounds.
- Privacy-conscious design.
- Appropriate signposting for young users.

## Design Priorities

The website should be welcoming, easy to navigate, informative and suitable for its intended audience.

## Limitations

ReConnectSphere provides information and signposting. It does not offer live chat, private messaging or emergency support.
